# SXcuts content and maintenance

Official source: https://sxcuts1123.square.site/ (retrieved 9 September 2026).
Gallery source: https://sxcuts1123.square.site/address . The source site's labels/routes are inconsistent.

Verified from official source: Saul; SXcuts; 53 Kitchener Street, St Ives NSW 2075; saulstar44@gmail.com; Instagram @sx.cuts_; testimonials by Hugo L. and Thomas I.; gallery photos. Testimonials use short excerpts and are attributed to the SXcuts website. They are not presented as verified Google reviews.

Current public directory phone was not included, pending confirmation. Opening hours were omitted because the official site displayed them next to unedited Square template contact details. No prices, durations, aggregate ratings, certifications, or business history were invented.

Edit `lib/site.ts` to set the direct Square appointment link or the verified Google Business Profile URL. Until a verified Google profile is supplied, the Google panel links to a precisely named Google Maps search. There is no live Google review feed; this requires a supported reviews provider/API connection. The site does not submit bookings or messages itself: it links to Square, Instagram, email and Maps.

Stack: React, Vinext, Tailwind CSS, installed shadcn/ui (Base UI) Sheet, Dialog, Accordion and Button primitives. Fonts (Anton and Manrope) are hosted locally. Photos are from the official SXcuts gallery.
